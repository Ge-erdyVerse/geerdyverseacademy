What is GeerdySense?

GeerdySense is a science-driven emotional safety device that detects stress, panic, fear, and danger through biosignals. It activates an intelligent AI companion designed to guide, protect, and support you during emotional extremes.

Why It Matters

The body shuts down before the mind understands danger. Panic blocks speech. Freeze stops movement. GeerdySense transforms emotional signals into awareness and protection.
